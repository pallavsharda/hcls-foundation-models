# 3.2 Digital pathology models

Pathology foundation models learn tile- and slide-level representations from whole-slide histopathology images. The applications include tumor detection, subtype classification, biomarker inference (including biomarkers historically considered invisible to the naked pathologist eye, such as MSI or HRD status from H&E alone), and survival prediction.
