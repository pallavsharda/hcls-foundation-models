# 3.4 Surgical video models

Video foundation models for the operating room and endoscopy suite learn temporal representations of intraoperative video. Use cases include phase recognition, instrument tracking, anatomy segmentation, and skill assessment. The category is younger than the others, but the existence of dedicated foundation models has changed the landscape from "computer vision applied to video" to something with the same kind of pretrained substrate the imaging and pathology categories have had for longer.
