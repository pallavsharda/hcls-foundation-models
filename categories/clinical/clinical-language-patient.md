# 3.3 Clinical language and patient models

Clinical language and patient models operate over messy, longitudinal frontline-care data — provider notes, lab orders and results, procedure notes, referrals, medications, claims — and increasingly, patient timelines. They typically support summarization, population health (cohort selection, risk prediction), coding, documentation, and clinical reasoning. Within this category three loose groupings recur in the literature: Clinical NLP models that learn from EHR text and normalize, summarize, or structure information; question-answering models trained for medical dialogue; and patient-trajectory models that learn from longitudinal patient data to represent how state changes over time and, in some cases, to predict what may happen next.
