# 3.1 Medical imaging models

The shift in medical imaging is from narrow single-task classifiers to broad, multi-task systems that handle detection, triage, and report generation in one pass. Many are vision-language models that take an image and produce free-text radiology reports, or accept clinician queries about findings and answer in natural language.
