# 1.1 Genomics models

DNA-input foundation models process genome sequences and learn the regulatory grammars, chromatin structure proxies, and evolutionary constraints that are not explicitly annotated in their training data. They are useful for variant interpretation, therapeutic-target discovery, synthetic-biology design, and clinical decision support in rare disease, exactly the places where small differences in nucleotide context can produce large differences in clinical outcome.
