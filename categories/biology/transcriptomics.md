# 1.2 Transcriptomics models

Transcriptomics foundation models learn cell-state manifolds, gene co-expression patterns, and regulatory relationships from single-cell and bulk RNA expression data. They are useful for drug-target discovery, mechanism-of-action studies, and cell-type annotation in atlases that increasingly span millions of cells.
