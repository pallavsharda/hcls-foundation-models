# 1.5 Antibody and biologics models

Antibody-specific language models learn the statistical grammar of heavy- and light-chain sequences from immune repertoire and therapeutic-antibody datasets. They are used in antibody design, humanization, affinity maturation, and developability and liability assessment, the parts of biologics drug development that used to depend almost entirely on wet-lab iteration.
