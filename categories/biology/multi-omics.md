# 1.6 Multi-omics and systems models

Multi-omics models integrate transcriptomics, proteomics, imaging, and other modalities to learn gene regulatory networks, cell-state manifolds, and disease trajectories simultaneously. The category is still emerging (there is not a single dominant foundation model yet), but the direction matters for any drug-target discovery program that wants pathway-level evidence rather than single-omic correlations.
