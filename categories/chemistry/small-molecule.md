# 2.1 Small-molecule models

These models work over SMILES strings or molecular graphs and are the workhorses of AI-driven medicinal chemistry. They are used for property-guided molecule generation, off-target liability prediction, and synthesizability assessment, often inside closed-loop platforms that pair generation with downstream screening.
